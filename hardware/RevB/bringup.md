### Rev-B Power Bring-Up

The Rev-B base board produced a valid 5 V rail, but the 3.3 V rail measured approximately 0.67 V.

The issue was traced to the buck converter inductor. A 3.9 mH inductor had been fitted instead of the required 3.9 µH part.

This is recorded as a component value error, not a confirmed PCB fault.

Rev-B testing is paused until the correct 3.9 µH inductors arrive.
